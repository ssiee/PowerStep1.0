Welcome to PowerStep

PowerStep is a sustainable energy project that converts kinetic energy from footsteps into usable electrical power. This website serves as an interface to showcase our thesis progress, data insights, and system overview.

About the System

PowerStep utilizes piezoelectric sensors embedded in tiles to collect kinetic energy from foot traffic. The system converts this into electrical energy, stores it in a battery, and uses an Arduino with sensors to control lighting and upload data to a server for monitoring.

Meet the Team

We are a group of Computer Engineering students from Cavite State University - Carmona Campus, developing this project as part of our thesis research on sustainable technology and smart systems.
